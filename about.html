About Jonas

Hi,

In hindsight, I realize that I’ve always followed my own path in life, making choices that defy social expectations. I’ve relocated between countries and cities and pursued many vocations. I never experienced what it was like to reside in one place for a lifetime, pursue one vocation, have a family, and raise children. Yet, like everyone else, Life challenged me with my own lessons.

Writing in various forms and for different purposes has always held a notable place in my life personally and professionally. After trying various genres, I’ve come to appreciate short form.

To me, the strength of flash writing is in the indirectness of the genre. Flash creative non-fiction embodies profound notions implicitly, using condensed form. The short form charges a piece with emotion and channels a pure message; this is how Life talks to us. Whether we understand the message depends on our ability to decipher ideas beyond the words. In flash writing, words of a piece act as frequencies for conveying meanings.

My essays and stories explore life through perceptions of the world’s mechanics beyond conventional wisdom, science, or religion. Started as a Christian devotee early on, throughout my adult life I’ve been studying the domain dubbed "esoteric." These studies led me to revelations and understanding that are reflected in my writing.

For decades I have written fragments, essays, and collected thoughts and observations. Now I am sharing my writing with the world. Above all, writing is a form of meditation for me, a practice of processing emotions, observations, and complexities of our earthly existence.

Jonas Rubinek
