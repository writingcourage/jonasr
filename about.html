About Jonas

Hi,

Looking back, I realize that I’ve always followed my own path in life, making choices that defy social expectations. I’ve relocated between countries and cities and pursued many vocations. I never experienced what it was like to live in one place, do one job, have a settled family life, or raise children. Yet, like everyone else, life presented me with its own upheavals and challenges.

In hindsight, writing in various forms and for different purposes has always held a notable place in my life, both personally and professionally. Although I’ve tried various genres, I’ve ultimately come to appreciate the short form the most.

To me, the strength of flash writing lies in its ability to convey profound ideas indirectly, in a condensed form using as few words as possible. The short form charges a piece with emotion and channels a pure message; this is how Life speaks to us. Whether we understand those messages depends on our ability to read between the lines and listen beyond the words, which are merely a medium for conveying meaning.

Throughout my life I have searched for a deeper understanding of how our world functions beyond conventional wisdom, science, or religion. Some people call this domain esoteric, but whatever label is applied, this search led me to insights that are reflected in my writing.

For decades I have written fragments, essays, and collected thoughts and observations. Now I am sharing my writing with the world. Above all, writing is a form of meditation for me, a practice of processing emotions, observations, and life’s complexities.

Jonas Rubinek
